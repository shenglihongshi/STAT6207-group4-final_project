01
Problem & objectiveWHY & WHAT

Second Talent fills 70–80% of its roles in tech. Recruiters still match CVs by hand — slow, and inconsistent across reviewers. We will build a reproducible matching baseline for tech roles: given one JD, return the Top-5 CVs with a short, evidence-based reason for each, then test it against pairs a recruiter has approved.

Not a production feature. The goal is to prove that a simple, explainable matcher can meet a senior recruiter's shortlist bar.
Top-5 hit rate ≥ 70% nDCG@5 ≥ 0.75 Blind-review agreement ≥ 80%

03
EvaluationHOW WE PROVE IT
  • Gold set: rule-generated candidates from 50 JDs × 100 CVs, recruiter-confirmed 50 tech positives + 50 hard negatives; 0–3 rubric (3 = strong yes, 2 = shortlist, 1 = borderline, 0 = no).
  • Reliability: first 20 pairs double-annotated; report Cohen's kappa; adjudicate disagreements; every label cites CV evidence.
  • Protocol: leave-one-JD-out to avoid leakage; compare against random, keyword, and embedding baselines.
  • Metrics: primary Top-5 hit rate and nDCG@5; secondary P@5, MRR, extraction F1, reason verifiability; error taxonomy: parsing / missed must-have / seniority / location-salary / hallucinated reason.
