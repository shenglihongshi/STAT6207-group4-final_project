Data Project Manager
Hong Kong, Hong Kong · Temporary · HKD 45,000 – 60,000 / month
Job Summary:
We are seeking an experienced and detail-oriented Data and Analytics Programme Manager to lead and oversee
the planning, execution, and delivery of our Enterprise data strategy across the organization. This individual will be
responsible for coordinating teams, resources, and timelines to ensure that data programs and projects are
delivered on time, within budget, and aligned with business goals. The ideal candidate will have a strong
background in data management, project management, change management and cross-functional collaboration.
Key Responsibilities:
• Lead the programme team to deliver a common data model, set up Hub and Spoke across domains, and deliver
standard reports – all from procure, design, build, test and go live, to handover to BAU support.
• Manage all programme deliverables and dependencies to other projects, collaborating with data engineers,
analytics team, domain owners, the wider Group Technology team and business stakeholders to achieve project
goals and deliverables.
• Create and maintain project plans, ensuring that milestones, timelines, and resources are well-defined and
managed effectively.
• Monitor budgets for programs and projects to ensure efficient use of resources and cost management.
• Manage suppliers to deliver the defined plan.
• Define and track key performance indicators (KPIs) to monitor progress.
• Drive program risk management by identifying potential risks, developing mitigation strategies, and resolving
issues promptly.
• Follow the client's decision making framework for architecture, data, AI, and financial approval.
• Build strong relationships with stakeholders, effectively communicating program status and results.
Requirements:
• Bachelor's degree (or higher) in data science, Computer Science, Business, Engineering, or a related field.
• 5+ years of experience in data programme management.
• Understanding of data architecture and analytics concepts.
• Familiarity with data analytics platforms and BI tools (e.g., Tableau, Power BI, Looker).
• Proven experience in managing large-scale, cross-functional data programs and projects.
• Excellent project management skills with proficiency in tools like Jira, Confluence or similar.
• Ability to meet multiple deadlines and milestones simultaneously.
• Strong communication, leadership, and organizational skills in a fast-paced environment.
• PMP, Agile, or other similar project management certifications are a plus.
• Previous experience in retail or a related industry managing complex data ecosystems is highly beneficial.
• Knowledge of market trends and emerging technologies such as blockchain, Internet of Things (IoT), Artificial
Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning, Data Streaming (Kafka) etc. would be a bonus.
