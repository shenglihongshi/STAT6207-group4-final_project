UI/UX Designer
Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia · Full-time · MYR 10,000 – 12,000
About the Role
We are looking for a creative and detail-oriented UI/UX Designer to craft intuitive, engaging, and visually appealing
digital experiences. The ideal candidate has a strong eye for design, understands user behavior, and can translate
business requirements into elegant design solutions that delight users and achieve product goals.
Key Responsibilities
• Collaborate with product managers, developers, and stakeholders to define user requirements and design
solutions.
• Create wireframes, user flows, prototypes, and high-fidelity mockups using tools such as Figma, Sketch, or
Adobe XD.
• Conduct user research and usability testing to identify pain points and validate design concepts.
• Translate brand identity and design systems into cohesive user interfaces across web and mobile platforms.
• Work closely with developers to ensure accurate implementation of designs.
• Continuously iterate and improve designs based on feedback and analytics.
• Stay up to date with UI/UX trends, tools, and best practices to ensure a modern and accessible experience.
Requirements
• Bachelor's degree in Design, HCI, or related field (or equivalent experience).
• 2–5 years of experience in UI/UX design or product design.
• Proficiency in design and prototyping tools (e.g., Figma, Adobe XD, Sketch, InVision).
• Strong understanding of user-centered design principles, interaction design, and accessibility standards.
• Experience working on responsive and mobile-first designs.
• Excellent communication and collaboration skills.
• Portfolio demonstrating design thinking, creativity, and attention to detail.
Nice-to-Have
• Experience with front-end development (HTML/CSS/JS).
• Familiarity with design systems and component libraries.
• Knowledge of motion design or microinteractions.
• Prior experience in agile/scrum environments.
