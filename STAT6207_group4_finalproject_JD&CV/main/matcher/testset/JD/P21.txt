Job Title: DevOps Engineer
Location: Shah Alam, Malaysia (In-person)
Salary: RM 6,000 – RM 9,000 per month
We are seeking a talented DevOps Engineer to join our dynamic team. You will be responsible for building,
automating, and managing cloud infrastructure, ensuring the high availability, performance, and reliability of a
market-leading platform.
Key Responsibilities
• Cloud Infrastructure: Collaborate with software developers to design, deploy, and manage scalable and reliable
infrastructure on AWS.
• Automation (CI/CD & IaC):
• Implement and manage automated CI/CD pipelines using tools like GitHub Actions, Jenkins, or CircleCI.
• Champion and execute Infrastructure-as-Code (IaC), with a strong emphasis on Ansible, Terraform, and AWS
CloudFormation.
• Write and maintain Python and Shell scripts to automate daily operational tasks and processes.
• Monitoring & Support: Monitor system performance, proactively troubleshoot complex issues, and implement
effective, long-term solutions to ensure optimal performance and high availability.
• Database Management: Take ownership of maintaining and deploying SQL servers, with a specific focus on
PostgreSQL and MySQL databases.
• Collaboration: Work with cross-functional teams to design and implement secure, efficient, and reliable
cloud-native solutions, continuously enhancing the infrastructure.
Required Qualifications (Must-Haves)
• Education: Diploma/Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field.
• AWS Experience: A minimum of 3 years of hands-on experience in cloud infrastructure deployment and
management, with a strong focus on AWS.
• IaC Expertise: Deep expertise in Infrastructure-as-Code (IaC), with mandatory, hands-on experience in Ansible;
strong, practical experience with Terraform and AWS CloudFormation.
• Containerization: Hands-on experience with containerization technologies, particularly Docker.
• CI/CD Tools: Proven experience with CI/CD concepts and tools (e.g., GitHub Actions, Jenkins, CircleCI).
• Scripting: Proficiency in Python and Shell scripting for automation.
• Database: Experience with the maintenance, deployment, and administration of PostgreSQL and MySQL
databases.
Preferred Qualifications (Nice-to-Have)
• AWS certifications (e.g., AWS Certified Solutions Architect, AWS Certified DevOps Engineer).
• Experience with microservices architecture (e.g., Serverless framework, Lambda).
• Familiarity with monitoring and logging tools (e.g., CloudWatch, Zabbix).
What We Offer
• Compensation: A competitive salary of RM 6,000.00 - RM 9,000.00 per month.
• Flexibility: Flexible work schedule.
• Benefits: Comprehensive health insurance.
• Growth: Clear opportunities for promotion and ongoing professional development.
