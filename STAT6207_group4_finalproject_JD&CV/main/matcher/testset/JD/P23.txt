Sr. Video Editor (Traditional & AI tools)
Remote · Full-time
Job Title: Senior Video Editor (AI & Post-Production)
Job Type: Full-time, Remote
Salary: Competitive, commensurate with experience
About the Opportunity
Our client is seeking a Senior Video Editor to join their high-performance creative team. This role is responsible for
producing high-impact, cinematic promotional videos, course trailers, and marketing campaigns. The ideal
candidate will be a master of traditional editing and an early adopter who has hands-on experience with the new
stack of AI-driven video tools.
Key Responsibilities
• Produce high-impact promotional videos, course trailers, and marketing campaigns that demonstrate cinematic
quality and strong narrative storytelling.
• Masterfully combine traditional editing software (e.g., Adobe Premiere Pro, CapCut Pro) with a new generation
of AI-driven tools (e.g., Sora 2, Veo 3, Higgsfield, Runway Gen-3, Dream Machine, Kling, Hailuo).
• Collaborate closely with the Creative, Growth, and Product teams to translate ambitious ideas into compelling,
story-driven video content.
• Actively research, test, and integrate new AI video technologies and techniques to keep the workflow innovative
and efficient.
• Ensure all video output meets an elite standard of visual and narrative quality, reflecting a high level of "taste"
and technical excellence.
Requirements
• Demonstrated mastery of professional, non-linear editing software (e.g., Adobe Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve,
After Effects).
• Verifiable, hands-on experience with advanced AI video tools (e.g., Sora 2, Veo 3, Higgsfield, Runway Gen-3,
etc.).
• Exceptional visual instincts with a deep, practical understanding of composition, rhythm, and pacing.
• A strong, high-quality portfolio that clearly demonstrates technical excellence, originality, and a high level of
creative "taste."
• A consistent drive to learn and stay ahead of creative and technological trends.
• Good command of English.
Bonus Points (Nice-to-Have)
• Experience in motion graphics, AI-assisted VFX, or 3D animation.
• A strong understanding of platform-native storytelling formats (e.g., YouTube, TikTok, Reels).
• A genuine interest in AI, technology, or the education sector.
Work Culture
The company's culture is modeled on a high-performance team. The environment is composed of individuals who
are top performers in their domain. The organization hires individuals with exceptional track records, trusts them to
deliver, and holds them to elite standards. New hires should expect a fast-paced environment and to collaborate
with other highly-skilled professionals. In return, the role offers accelerated learning, rapid growth, and the
opportunity to create work that stands out in the industry.
How to Apply
Interested candidates are invited to submit their resume and a portfolio for immediate consideration.
