Singapore, Singapore · full time · SGD 9,000 – 20,000
Deployment Strategist - Role Overview
As a Deployment Strategist at the client, you'll own the successful delivery of AI solutions for enterprise customers
- turning ambitious AI initiatives into real, measurable outcomes.
You'll lead complex implementations from early scoping through production launch, working closely with customers
and internal teams to align on requirements, timelines, and success metrics. You'll ensure that each deployment is
well-coordinated, executed on time, and set up to scale.
This role is ideal for people who enjoy operating at the intersection of technology, execution, and customer
collaboration, and who take pride in making complex projects land smoothly.
What You'll Do
• Own and lead delivery of the client's platform and AI agents for enterprise customers
• Scope requirements, success metrics, and integration plans for Pilots and onboardings
• Coordinate platform implementation, integrations, and agent refinement toward production launches
• Align Product, Engineering, and customer teams on deliverables, timelines, and SLAs
• Monitor AI agent performance in production and drive follow-up improvements
• Identify and develop new AI use cases with customers to expand adoption and impact
• Track progress, manage risks, and remove blockers throughout the delivery lifecycle
What We're Looking For
• Comfort working with technical concepts such as APIs, data flows, and system integrations
• Ability to manage multiple stakeholders and keep complex efforts moving forward
• High-agency mindset: you don't wait for perfect requirements and are comfortable creating clarity, proposing
direction, and driving decisions
• Proactive, hands-on mindset and interest in AI-driven products
• Strong communication skills, project management, organization
• Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or a related technical field or equivalent experience
Nice to Have
• Engineering background or prior hands-on technical experience
• Background in management consulting, strategy consulting, or technical consulting, with experience leading
problem discovery, structuring ambiguous problems, and working with senior stakeholders
• Experience working in a high-growth or early-stage startup environment
