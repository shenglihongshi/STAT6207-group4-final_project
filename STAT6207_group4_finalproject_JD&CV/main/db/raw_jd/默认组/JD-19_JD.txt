Position summary: The client's Information Services (IS) department embraces a culture of customer service excellence and all members of the department are expected to move this agenda forward. To that end, the Network Engineer is expected to recognize that the IS department is a service organization first and foremost and will be evaluated on this requirement equal in importance to the technical or operational responsibilities outlined later in this document.

The Network Engineer, as part of the IS Network Services & Engineering group, is primarily responsible to provide LAN, WAN, and WLAN Tier 3 support during Asia Pacific hours. Specific duties and responsibilities include, but are not limited to, the following:

Primary position responsibilities:

• Day-to-day technical support of network infrastructure at the firm's datacenters and at firm office premises globally, ensuring a high level of efficiency in problem resolution and uptime
• Configure firm LAN, WLAN and WAN environments
• Monitor network performance and integrity
• Troubleshooting/issue management of firm network infrastructure
• Perform network maintenance, upgrades and refreshes
• Perform wireless surveys, analyze results and remediation
• Manage and support user remote access (Palo Alto Prisma/Global Protect)
• Assist with regional office build outs and upgrades
• Monitor, measure and analyze data and trends against performance SLAs
• Coordinate with and participate in activities with vendors, as required
• Provide clear and grammatically correct system and end user documentation, emails, procedures, and status (both verbal and written)
• Comply with all department policies, standards and procedures
• Participate in Change Control and Change Management
• Respond to administrative requests and responsibilities in a timely manner
• Assist Management in administrative tasks and vendor management

Other responsibilities: In addition to performance of the primary position responsibilities, the Network Engineer (Asia) may be required to perform a combination of the following supportive functions:

• Assist with the deployment and support of systems infrastructure
• Participate in Change Control and Change Management
• Respond to administrative requests and responsibilities in a timely manner
• Assist Management in administrative tasks and vendor management
