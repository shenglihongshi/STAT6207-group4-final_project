About the Role

Join the Research Compliance team at a fast-growing global knowledge services firm, supporting the delivery of expert insights to top-tier clients worldwide. As a Research Compliance Associate, you'll help ensure that all research activities meet internal standards and international regulatory requirements.

You'll report to the Research Compliance Manager (based in Hong Kong) and collaborate closely with Research, Legal, and Client Protection teams, as well as other key business stakeholders.

What You'll Do
• Review expert profiles and project details to ensure compliance with company and regulatory guidelines
• Proofread and format written outputs (transcripts) for accuracy and compliance
• Identify and help resolve any legal or compliance flags with internal teams
• Deliver compliance training sessions to research and cross-functional teams
• Support the development and maintenance of internal compliance policies
• Work independently and proactively in a fast-paced, high-growth environment

What We're Looking For
• Bachelor's degree in any field with strong academic performance
• 0-1 year of experience in a compliance, legal, or paralegal function (recent graduates are welcome)
• Exceptional attention to detail, especially in proofreading and formatting
• Strong time management, prioritization, and organizational skills
• Fluent in written and spoken English; additional language skills are a plus
• Demonstrated extracurricular involvement and leadership experience
• Self-starter with a proactive, can-do mindset

Career Progression
• Years 0-2: Ensure research projects meet legal and compliance standards
• Years 2+: Advance to Senior Research Compliance Associate, leading initiatives and mentoring team members
• Years 3+: Progress to Research Compliance Manager, overseeing regional strategy and driving innovation

Compensation & Benefits
