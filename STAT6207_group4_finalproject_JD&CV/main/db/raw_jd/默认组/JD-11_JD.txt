Position: Senior Creative Designer

We are looking for a passionate and imaginative Senior Creative Designer to join our growing team. If you're someone who thrives in a fast-paced, collaborative environment and knows how to bring ideas to life with compelling visuals, this could be your next big move.

Key Responsibilities

• Collaborate with clients to understand their branding, marketing, and creative needs.
• Translate ideas into impactful design concepts through research, mood boards, and creative exploration.
• Develop initial layouts, illustrations, and visual concepts in alignment with brand guidelines.
• Refine and finalize creative assets based on client feedback and internal review.
• Ensure all deliverables meet aesthetic standards and support business/marketing goals.
• Work closely with the Marketing, Business Support, and Search & Advertising teams to meet design requirements on time.
• Stay current with design trends, industry innovations, and best practices to continuously elevate creative output.
• Provide strategic input and suggest creative enhancements to optimize campaigns.
• Evaluate the effectiveness of creative materials and iterate based on performance data.
• Participate in client meetings to align on direction, present ideas, and discuss brand evolution.
• Be proactive in identifying visual improvements or additional design needs for ongoing projects.

What We're Looking For

• A Diploma, Degree, or equivalent certification in Art, Design, Creative Multimedia, or related fields.
• Minimum 1 year of experience in graphic design or creative media; agency experience is a plus.
• Strong portfolio demonstrating a range of creative work across various media.
• Proficiency in Adobe Creative Suite (Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign); video editing and animation skills are a bonus.
• Familiarity with template-based CMS tools is advantageous.
• Creative flair, attention to detail, and an eye for typography, layout, and visual hierarchy.
• Understanding of cross-cultural design nuances is a plus.
• Willingness to work onsite at Bandar Sunway.
• Ideal for junior to senior executives in advertising, design, or media planning.
