About the Role

We are looking for a Senior/Lead Data Engineer to design, build, and optimize large-scale data systems that power critical business decisions. This role is 75% focused on data engineering (data pipelines, architectures, automation) and 25% supporting data science (ML workflows, advanced analytics).

You'll work with technologies like Python, Airflow, Kubernetes, and AWS to deliver scalable and reliable data solutions.

Key Responsibilities

• Architect, develop, and maintain scalable data infrastructure (data lakes, pipelines, metadata repositories).
• Collaborate with data scientists to support data modeling, ML workflows, and experimentation.
• Build and optimize large-scale batch and real-time processing systems.
• Automate workflows with Python, Apache Airflow, and AWS services.
• Manage data storage and compute resources using AWS (S3, Glue, EC2, Lambda).
• Implement strong testing and validation procedures to ensure accuracy and reliability.
• Stay up to date with best practices and emerging technologies in data engineering and data science.

Required Skills

• Bachelor's degree in Software Engineering or related field.
• Strong experience in Python (pandas, pyspark), Apache Airflow, and AWS services (Glue, S3, EC2, Lambda).
• Hands-on with Kubernetes/Docker for containerized environments.
• Experience with big data/columnar databases (Athena, Redshift, Hive/Hadoop, Vertica).
• Familiarity with Git and CI/CD pipelines (Jenkins, CircleCI, AWS CodePipeline).
• Proven ability to design and manage data architectures for large-scale operations.
• Experience collaborating with data science teams for feature engineering and ML support.

Nice-to-have

• Experience with AWS Sagemaker or Databricks.
• Knowledge of RDBMS (MySQL, PostgreSQL) and NoSQL (DynamoDB, Redis).
• Familiarity with Kafka, RabbitMQ for event streaming.
• Exposure to BI tools (Tableau, Looker, PowerBI).
• Experience with monitoring/logging tools (ELK, Datadog).
• Understanding of data privacy and security practices.
