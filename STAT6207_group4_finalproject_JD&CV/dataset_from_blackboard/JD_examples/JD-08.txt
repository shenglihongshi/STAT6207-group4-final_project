Job Title: AI Artist / Video Editor (Creative Agency)

Location: Remote

Job Type: Full-time

About the Opportunity

Our client is a modern creative agency specializing in content for social media. They help well-known brands connect with audiences by combining AI, CGI, and VFX to create visually compelling stories.

They are seeking an AI Artist/Video Editor to join their team. This role involves using a combination of AI tools, professional video editing software, and creative skills to produce high-impact social media content. You will collaborate with the creative team to transform concepts into polished videos and AI-enhanced visuals.

Key Responsibilities

• Content Creation: Craft high-quality videos for social media platforms, leveraging AI tools and professional editing software to deliver visually striking content.
• AI-Driven Innovation: Use AI platforms (e.g., Midjourney, Kling) to generate and enhance creative assets. Seamlessly integrate AI-generated imagery, animations, and effects into video projects.
• Creative Collaboration: Partner with the creative director and account servicing team to translate client briefs into engaging visual stories that align with brand goals and platform requirements.
• Post-Production Mastery: Manage all aspects of video post-production, including editing, color grading, and sound design, to ensure professional, polished deliverables.
• Trend Awareness: Stay current on social media trends, AI advancements, and video editing techniques to produce fresh, relevant content.
• Project Management: Manage multiple projects, delivering assets on time while upholding specific creative and technical standards.
• Feedback Integration: Efficiently incorporate client and team feedback, iterating to meet project goals and deadlines.
• Platform Optimization: Tailor content specifically for social media platforms (primarily Instagram), optimizing for format, aspect ratio, and audience engagement.

Qualifications

• Experience: 2+ years of professional experience in video editing, motion graphics, or AI-driven content creation, ideally within a creative agency, marketing, or social media setting. (Junior candidates with exceptionally strong portfolios may be considered.)
• Technical Expertise: High proficiency in video editing software (e.g., After Effects). Hands-on experience with AI tools (e.g., Midjourney, Kling, Freepik, Seedance, Seedream). Must understand how to use AI to enhance the creative process.
• Portfolio (Mandatory): A standout portfolio is required, showcasing video editing, motion graphics, and/or AI-generated content. The portfolio must include social media or brand-driven projects.
• Skills:
  • A sharp eye for aesthetics, storytelling, and visual design.
  • Ability to thrive in a dynamic, fast-paced environment and adapt to evolving client needs and new technologies.
  • Strong communication skills, with the ability to work remotely and collaborate effectively.
  • Must be able to manage multiple projects and meet tight deadlines without sacrificing quality.
  • Requires a deep knowledge of social media platforms, trends, and audience behaviors.

What We Offer

• Compensation: A base salary of HKD $10,000 - $15,000 per month, plus performance-based bonuses.
• Flexibility: A remote work arrangement with flexible hours, focusing on results.
• Impact: The opportunity to work on high-profile campaigns for top brands, creating content seen by millions.
• Tools & Innovation: Access to cutting-edge AI tools and creative software, with opportunities to experiment.
• Growth: A role in a team where your skills will help shape the future of social media content, with room for professional advancement.
