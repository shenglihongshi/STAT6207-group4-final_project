Contract: full_time

Tax manager

1. Coordinate among local finance team, external tax agencies, local legal team to support tax audit cases across the client's group entities
2. Visit and liaise with in-charge ARs and tax auditors in the tax office periodically, i.e. once or twice every week, with the assistance of external tax agencies for the purpose of relationship building, tax audit cases communication and other daily tax matters
3. Conduct tax health check for each entity with the assistance of external tax agencies, advise and execute improvement and monitor the progress
4. Use the tool advised by the external agencies during tax health checks and perform periodical tax reconciliation
5. Organize monthly/quarterly tax recon meetings, create and improve recon procedures
6. Monitor the progress of transfer pricing documentation of each entity; facilitate the progress if they are behind the schedule set by the tax director
7. Provide Indonesian tax training and tax knowledge to the local team and also to headquarters
8. Report directly to each finance director, mainly on audit cases, with a dotted line to the tax director for other tax matters

No need to speak Mandarin.

Must-haves:
1) Have worked for the Big Four accounting firms: KPMG, PwC, Deloitte or EY
2) Know the manufacturing industry well
3) Have government connections
