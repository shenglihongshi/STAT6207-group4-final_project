AI CV Matching Project
Student briefing: the project and your role
1 Why this project
Second Talent places talent for clients through recruitment, secondment and EOR.
We are building an HRIS platform. One feature is AI candidate matching: a client describes a role,
and the system suggests the best-fit profiles.
70–80% of our roles are tech (AI, front-end, back-end, full-stack), so the project focuses on tech.
Recruiters match CVs by hand today, which takes a lot of time. AI can shortlist faster.
2 The goal
Give the system a job description (JD) and get back a ranked shortlist of CVs, each
with a short reason, that an experienced Second Talent recruiter would agree with.

6 What recruiters look at
Weight
Factors
Highest
Hard skills overlap (must-have vs nice-to-have) · Years of relevant experience
High
Seniority level (junior / mid / senior / lead) · Expected salary vs budget
Medium
Industry / domain background · Job stability (tenure, gaps) · Education or certificates
(only if the role needs them)
Filters
Location, time zone, remote eligibility · Notice period · English and other languages
These weights are a rough guide. We'll agree the final numbers with the recruitment team.
7 Data & tools
Manatal (our ATS): the main source of CVs (PDF) and JDs. We also source from LinkedIn Recruiter and
Juicebox.
Starting sheet: "Manatal Examples – JD & CV (anonymised)" has 50 JDs and 100 CVs, with no
match labels yet.
Target: 100–200 tech JDs and about 50 good-match CV–JD pairs.
Tools: Claude for extraction and masking. Python, pandas, embeddings and Google Sheets for
labelling.
Privacy rule: mask names, emails, phones, addresses, photos and LinkedIn URLs before any data
leaves Second Talent. Check by hand. Nothing gets shared until Eric approves it.
