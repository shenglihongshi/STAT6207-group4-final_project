Marketing and Sales Manager (Singapore and MY):

I. Job Description

Market Development and Client Acquisition
• Responsible for market development in the commercial fit-out sector (e.g., office spaces, commercial spaces, factory renovations, etc.) and identifying potential client leads.
• Acquire new clients through various channels, including trade shows, industry events, and online platforms, to establish long-term partnerships.

End-to-End Sales Management
• Achieve sales targets, including client onboarding, contract negotiations, project handover, and payment collection.
• Participate in the bidding process, prepare qualification documents, and coordinate with the technical team to complete design proposals and quotations.

Client Relationship Management
• Maintain relationships with existing clients, conduct regular follow-ups, and resolve post-sales issues to enhance client satisfaction and repeat business.
• Handle client complaints and coordinate with departments such as design and engineering to optimize service processes.
• Coordinate cross-departmental resources (e.g., design, construction, procurement) to ensure efficient project implementation.

II. Job Requirements

Education and Major
• Associate degree or higher; preference given to candidates with a background in Marketing, Engineering Management, or related fields.
• Language Requirements: Chinese and English

Work Experience
• 5+ years of market experience in the commercial fit-out industry; preference given to candidates with expertise in MEP fit-out projects for data centers in Singapore.
• Preference given to candidates with Business Development (BD) experience at large design, interior decoration, or construction firms.
