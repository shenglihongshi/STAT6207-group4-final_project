Remote · Full-time
Role Overview
As a Business Development hire, you'll be responsible for driving revenue growth through new client acquisition
and partnership building, primarily focused on promoting the client's Employer of Record (EOR) and remote hiring
solutions.
You'll identify prospects, manage the sales pipeline, and build strong relationships with HR leaders, founders, and
global companies looking to expand in Asia and beyond.

• Drive end-to-end sales for the client's EOR and remote hiring services.
• Identify and engage potential clients (startups, SMEs, global companies) expanding into Asia or hiring remotely.
• Build and manage a strong sales pipeline through outbound and inbound channels.
• Conduct consultative sales calls to understand client hiring and compliance needs.
• Collaborate with internal teams (operations, legal, talent) to deliver customized solutions.
• Develop and execute business growth strategies, including partnerships and channel collaborations.
• Stay updated with global employment laws, remote hiring trends, and competitor landscape.

HR tech.
• Strong understanding of the Employer of Record model and global hiring compliance.
• Proven track record of closing B2B deals and managing enterprise or startup clients.
• Excellent communication, presentation, and negotiation skills.
• Self-driven, result-oriented, and comfortable in a fast-paced, remote-first environment.
• Experience working across the US or Europe market is a plus.
