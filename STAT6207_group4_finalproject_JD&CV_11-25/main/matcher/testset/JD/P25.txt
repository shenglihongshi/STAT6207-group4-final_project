HR (Part-time)
Singapore, Singapore · Part-time (permanent) · SGD 3,000 – 4,000 / month
Role Overview
This role is ideal for an HR professional who enjoys end-to-end HR work within a lean structure. You will partner
closely with the Regional HR Director and support daily HR operations, employee lifecycle matters, policy
administration, and regional coordination across multiple countries.
This is a permanent part-time position with fixed afternoon working hours, offering stability, exposure to regional
HR, and the opportunity to work directly with senior HR leadership.
Key Responsibilities
• Handle HR documentation including onboarding, confirmation, transfers, exits, work passes, and employee
records.
• Maintain HR databases and ensure accuracy of employee information.
• Support HR policy rollout, compliance tracking, and documentation.
• Assist with job postings, screening, interview arrangements, and pipeline coordination.
• Liaise with internal stakeholders to ensure smooth hiring processes.
• Coordinate HR initiatives and information flow between APAC markets.
• Prepare reports, slides, and HR data for the Regional HR Director.
• Help execute engagement activities, performance review cycles, and feedback collection.
• Support HR system enhancements, SOP development, and policy updates.
Requirements
• Diploma/Degree in Human Resources, Business, or related field.
• 2–5 years of HR experience, ideally covering HR operations and generalist work.
• Exposure to regional HR matters is preferred.
• Strong organisational skills, attention to detail, and ability to work independently.
• Proficient in MS Office (Excel, PowerPoint).
• Comfortable interacting with stakeholders across markets and cultures.
