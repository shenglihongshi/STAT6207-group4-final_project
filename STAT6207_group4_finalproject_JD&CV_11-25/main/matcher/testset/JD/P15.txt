Business Analyst
Singapore, Singapore · Full-time · 5,000 – 10,000
Job Title: Business Analyst
Location: Taiwan
Contract Duration: 1 Year (Renewable based on performance and project needs)
Job Summary:
We are seeking a highly motivated and detail-oriented Junior Business Analyst to join our dynamic team. This
entry-level role offers an exciting opportunity to contribute to key business initiatives by analyzing requirements,
documenting processes, and supporting the delivery of impactful solutions. The Junior Business Analyst will work
closely with stakeholders across different departments and collaborate with technical teams to ensure projects
align with business objectives.
Responsibilities:
Requirements Elicitation and Analysis: Assist in gathering business requirements through various techniques such
as interviews, workshops, and surveys. Document and analyze business needs, functional and non-functional
requirements clearly and concisely. Support the creation of user stories, use cases, and process flows. Assist in
prioritizing requirements based on business value and feasibility.
Documentation and Communication: Create and maintain project documentation, including requirements
specifications, process maps, and meeting minutes. Communicate effectively with stakeholders at all levels to
clarify requirements and provide updates. Support the development of presentations and reports to communicate
findings and recommendations.
Process Improvement: Assist in analyzing existing business processes to identify areas for improvement and
optimization. Contribute to the development of proposed solutions and process changes. Support the
implementation and monitoring of process improvements.
Collaboration and Support: Work closely with cross-functional teams, including developers, testers, and project
managers, to ensure alignment and successful project delivery. Provide support during testing phases by clarifying
requirements and assisting with issue resolution. Participate in project meetings and contribute to discussions.
Data Analysis and Reporting: Assist in collecting and analyzing data to support business decisions. Contribute to
the creation of basic reports and dashboards. Support the identification of data trends and insights.
Learning and Development: Actively seek opportunities to learn and develop business analysis skills and
methodologies. Stay updated on industry best practices and emerging trends. Participate in training and
development programs as required.
Qualifications:
Bachelor's degree in Business Administration, Information Technology, Economics, or a related field. Strong
analytical and problem-solving skills. Excellent written and verbal communication skills. Ability to effectively
communicate technical concepts to non-technical audiences. Strong attention to detail and organizational skills.
Proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Visio or similar process mapping tools). Ability to
work independently and as part of a team. A proactive and results-oriented approach. A strong desire to learn and
contribute to the success of the organization.
Preferred Qualifications (Not Mandatory):
Familiarity with Agile or Waterfall methodologies. Basic understanding of database concepts and SQL. Experience
with requirements management tools (e.g., Jira, Confluence, Azure DevOps). Previous internship or project
experience in a business analysis or related role.
