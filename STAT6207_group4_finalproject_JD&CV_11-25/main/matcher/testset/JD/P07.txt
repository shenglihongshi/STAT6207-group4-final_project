Hiring: APAC Marketing Manager (Hong Kong)
We're looking for an experienced APAC Marketing Manager to drive regional growth across key markets, with a
strong focus on communications, events and field marketing. This is a high-ownership role responsible for building
and scaling our APAC marketing foundation.
This role is ideal for someone who understands the Hong Kong market deeply, combines strong strategic thinking
with hands-on execution, and can navigate fast-paced, cross-functional regional environments effectively.
Key Responsibilities
• Lead end-to-end marketing initiatives across PR, content, campaigns, partnerships, and events.
• Build and lead executive, customer, partner, and community events to strengthen brand presence and
ecosystem engagement.
• Translate global messaging into compelling local narratives and marketing assets that resonate with the market.
• Support market expansion and go-to-market initiatives in close collaboration with regional GMs and HQ teams.
• Partner cross-functionally with sales, product, partnerships, and global marketing teams to drive regional growth
and execution.
Qualifications

environments.
• Strong expertise in field marketing, PR, content development, ecosystem engagement, and integrated campaign
execution.
• Solid understanding of product positioning, messaging, and enterprise go-to-market strategies.
• Prior exposure to AI, SaaS, cloud, or broader technology industries is highly preferred.
Language Requirements
• Fluent in English, Cantonese
Profile
• Highly self-driven with strong ownership
• Able to operate at both strategic and execution levels
• Comfortable working in ambiguity and building from 0 to 1
• Strong communicator with structured thinking and business acumen
